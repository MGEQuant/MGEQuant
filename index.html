The MGE research pipeline

Most ideas don't survive the pipeline. That is the point of it.

  1. Idea
  2. Hypothesis
  3. Prototype
  4. Backtest
  5. Forward test
  6. Break / refine
  7. Document

Every step is checked against a random-entry control and out-of-sample data, and we correct our own numbers in public when we find a mistake.

What passing means

  • Enough trades to mean something: roughly 300 or more signals a year.
  • Beats random entry taken with the same stops, targets and timing.
  • Survives real costs: commissions and slippage included, not added later.
  • Holds out of sample, on data the rules never saw.

Backtest vs. forward test

A mechanical backtest tests one fixed version of a rule. It can find no edge while the same tool stays useful in a trader's hands, read their own way. We publish both sides.
