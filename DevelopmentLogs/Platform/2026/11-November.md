## November 4 — MGE Labs: sign-up proves the email address, and your data can leave with you

Sign-up now asks for an email address only. The account cannot be used until you open a link sent to that address and choose a password there, so nobody can open an account with an address they do not own. Sign-ups are still by invitation while the beta runs.

Profile > Account has a "Download my data" button: your account data in one file. If you ask us to delete your account, it is removed completely, and a confirmation email is sent.

Smaller changes: records of wrong-password attempts no longer store email addresses. In the light theme, grey text was too pale to read comfortably; a check found 61 spots below the common 4.5:1 contrast standard, and now there are none. The dark theme got quieter panels, and numbers line up in columns.

## November 9–10 — Support email lands where questions are answered

Mail sent to support@mgequant.com now arrives in the same support inbox as the form inside the app. Each email gets its own reference number, and when you reply to our answer it stays in the same conversation instead of starting a new one.

A person answers every message; nothing is answered automatically. The privacy page was updated the same day to say who handles support email.

## November 14 — Beta testers wanted

The Labs page now says who we are looking for as testers and what testers receive. One button opens an email to us with five short questions already filled in, so nobody has to guess what to write. We reply within 5 working days.

## November 18 — Journal: a month calendar, and a page you can lay out your way

The journal now has a month calendar. Each day shows its number of trades, the dollars and the result in R. Click a day and the list below shows only that day's trades.

The calendar shows no win rate: it is there to show when you traded and how those days went, not to score you.

You can now choose how the journal page reads: one long page, a side list of sections, or one section at a time. On a desktop the side menu can be made narrower to give the trades more room.

## November 19 — A way back in if you lose your phone and your recovery codes

Two-step sign-in protects the account, and recovery codes get you in if your phone is lost. If both are gone, there is now a protected way back in. It is slower on purpose, because it has to be safe against someone pretending to be you. Your plan, payments and trades are never touched; only the sign-in is reset.

## November 21 — Website: a person behind the help bubble, outside scripts blocked, plainer privacy words

The help bubble on every page has a working "Talk to a person" button. It shows our support email with buttons to copy the address and a short starter message. A person reads every message and replies by email.

Every page of the website is now protected against outside ads or scripts being slipped in. Nothing you see changed.

The privacy page explains our hosting provider's technical log in plain words first. The full list of what it holds is behind a fold-out, and it says how long it is kept: up to 7 days.
