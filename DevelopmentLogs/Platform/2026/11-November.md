## November 4 — MGE Labs: sign-up proves the email address, and your data can leave with you

Sign-up now asks for an email address only. The account cannot be used until you open a link sent to that address and choose a password there, so nobody can open an account with an address they do not own. Sign-ups are still by invitation while the beta runs.

Profile > Account has a "Download my data" button: your account data in one file. If you ask us to delete your account, it is removed completely, and a confirmation email is sent.

Smaller changes: records of wrong-password attempts no longer store email addresses. In the light theme, grey text was too pale to read comfortably; a check found 61 spots below the common 4.5:1 contrast standard, and now there are none. The dark theme got quieter panels, and numbers line up in columns.

## November 9–10 — Support email lands where questions are answered

Mail sent to support@mgequant.com now arrives in the same support inbox as the form inside the app. Each email gets its own reference number, and when you reply to our answer it stays in the same conversation instead of starting a new one.

A person answers every message; nothing is answered automatically. The privacy page was updated the same day to say who handles support email.
