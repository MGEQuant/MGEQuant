Indicators read the market and draw on the chart. None of them place orders.

## November 2 — TSI Divergence: the clock change moved the New York window

TSI Divergence only marks setups inside its session windows: Asia, London and New York.

When the clocks changed on November 1, the New York window opened an hour early on a PC whose clock did not match the time the indicator expected. Nothing crashed and nothing warned. The panel simply looked at the wrong hour.

The windows now follow NinjaTrader's own time setting instead of the PC clock, so they stay right on any PC and through every clock change. It is the same lesson as the IB 25/75 check in October: never assume what the clock on the PC says.

The chart title now shows only the indicator's name.
